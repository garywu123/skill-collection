# Discovery Operation

Use the [discovery notes template](../assets/discovery-notes-template.md).

1. Establish users/problems, outcomes, scope/non-goals, business rules,
   safeguards, success measures, assumptions, contradictions, and open decisions.
2. Ask only questions that can change scope, behavior, priority, safety, or
   success criteria; at most five independent numbered questions per round.
3. Recommend an answer only when evidence supports it, and state its consequence.
4. Append decisions, rationale, rejected alternatives, changed conclusions, and
   remaining frontier. Supersede earlier decisions instead of rewriting history.
5. Mark `Ready for Review` only when uncertainty is resolved or bounded by an
   explicit assumption that cannot materially change the proposed product.

Stay at product altitude. Technology, storage, APIs, components, and frameworks
belong elsewhere.
